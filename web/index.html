HOW TO PLAY

Eat every pellet in the maze to clear the level. Each pellet is worth 10 points. Don't let a ghost touch you.

Power pellets turn the ghosts blue for a few seconds, and then you can eat them: 200, 400, 800 and 1600 points in a row. The POWER bar above the maze shows how long you have.

Bonus fruit appears twice per level. It's worth 100 points on level 1 and up to 2000 later on.

Score 10,000 points for an extra life. The side exits are tunnels to the other side of the maze.

CONTROLS

Computer: arrow keys or W A S D to move, P to pause, M for sound.
Phone: swipe on the maze, or use the arrow pad under it.
